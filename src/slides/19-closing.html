III / EXACT GRAPH GENERATION

From edge arrivals to dynamic random graphs

Three take-home messages.

01

Exact, sorting-free generation

Generate proposals, not a scan of all candidate pairs.

02

Change-sensitive dynamic updates

Poisson additions + geometric deletions.
The cleanest bounds hold for NR and GRG.

03

Minimum expected edge recourse

Preserve the target laws while changing as few edges as possible in expectation.
